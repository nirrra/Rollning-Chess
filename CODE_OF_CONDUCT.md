# Code Of Conduct

Rolling Chess is a small technical community. Keep discussion focused on the project and treat other contributors with respect.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear and constructive.
- Assume good intent when asking for clarification.
- Critique code, rules, tests, and designs rather than people.
- Keep rule debates grounded in reproducible positions or written specifications.
- Accept maintainer decisions after discussion has converged.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Repeated off-topic comments after being asked to stop.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive issue or pull request activity.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments and may close issues or pull requests that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may lead to a ban from participation in the repository.

## Reporting

If you see conduct that needs maintainer attention, open a private communication channel with the repository owner if available, or flag the behavior in the relevant GitHub thread with a concise explanation.
